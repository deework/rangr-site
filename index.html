THE RIGHT NUMBERS FOR YOUR SETUP

One car.
Different setups.
All saved.

Your daily drive and your car with a trailer do not have to share the same consumption figure.

Save a profile for each car, or for a trailer, roof box or other accessories. Each profile keeps its own capacity and consumption. Switch profiles to bring those values into your calculation.

You choose the consumption for each setup. Rangr calculates from the values you enter.
